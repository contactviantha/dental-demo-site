Meet the Doctor

Experienced, Gentle & Patient-First

Dr. S. Rajan has been practicing dentistry for over 15 years. He completed his BDS from Tamil Nadu Government Dental College and MDS in Prosthodontics from SRM Dental College, Chennai.

His philosophy is simple: treat every patient the way you would treat your own family. He is known for explaining every step before performing it — so patients always know what is happening and why.
